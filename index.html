How to play

Snake

Steer with arrows or WASD. Each food gives 1 point. The walls wrap around. You lose when you hit yourself.

Memory Match

Score starts at 1000 and drops 40 per move. Result shows moves and time.

Reaction Test

Click too early and the round repeats. Your score is the average of 5 rounds, lower is better.

Tic-Tac-Toe

You are X. The computer plays well but not perfectly. Max score is 15.

2048

Merge equal tiles. The game ends when no move is left or you reach 2048.

Sky Hop

Each pipe you pass is 1 point. Touching a pipe or the ground ends the run.

Whack-a-Mole

Click the mole to score. Clicking an empty hole counts as a miss. Result shows accuracy.

FAQ

Where are my scores saved?

In your browser on this device. Clearing site data resets them.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Snake and 2048 use swipes, the other games use taps.

How do I change the look?

Use the colored dots in the header or press T.

Can I add my own game?

Add a function to js/games.js, a name in LABEL in js/main.js, and a card in index.html.
